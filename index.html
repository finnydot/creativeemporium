About

I build stories from the ground up, whether that means mapping out multi-book sci-fi sagas, writing and producing the tracks behind them, or directing the visual worlds that bring them to life. For me, the medium is just a different tool for the same job.

When I'm creating under the moniker Finny, the workflow is hands-on and obsessive—handling everything from the narrative structure down to the final master.

I lean into modern tools across AI video, multi-track audio, and prose to execute ideas quickly and uncompromisingly. If a world is worth building, it's worth seeing all the way through to the final cut.
