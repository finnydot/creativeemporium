World 03 · Recovered Codex

The Ghost of Alexander

Some secrets are buried deeper than a king.

Alexander is four miles ahead.

Behind him, a young clerk counts grain, copies orders, and learns that a name written on the wrong page can condemn someone—or save them.

Around him, lovers hide in the spaces power leaves unwatched. Sealed letters travel after dark. The accounts balance. Something is wrong.

Alexander never speaks.
His empire speaks for him.

Six charred papyrus rolls preserve what one clerk dared to record—and what he refused to surrender.

One finished manuscript. In revision.

World 04 · The Saga

SPECIMEN 884

One man wakes on an alien world. An eon stands between him and home.

Julian Reyes has lost his wife. Now he has lost Earth.

On Asylia, survival means repairing machines he barely understands, trusting strangers with wounds of their own, and discovering why an ancient system has singled him out as Specimen 884.

But Julian’s arrival is only the beginning.

Across generations, survival becomes civilization. Families inherit the consequences of old sacrifices. Worlds depend on one another. And the people capable of saving millions face a temptation more dangerous than any enemy: believing that the power to save a life grants the right to decide it.

Spanning nine books and over an eon, SPECIMEN 884 follows engineers, explorers, rulers, and an ancient observer through grief, war, alien frontiers, and a universe that offers no promise of safety.

At its heart is a question that grows more urgent with every life saved:

When survival demands a choice, who gets to make it—and who gets to say no?

    A SPECIMEN 884 COMPANION

    The Last Thing We Keep

    He spent an eon keeping the lives of others. Now he asks someone to understand his own.

    When Bob makes a request Sam cannot bear to accept, he offers her something no one has ever received: the memories beneath his archives.

    Together, they enter a history of stolen lives, unexpected kindness, and the moment an ancient observer began to care.

    An intimate companion to SPECIMEN 884, The Last Thing We Keep asks what we owe the people we love—and what remains when we can no longer hold on.

    A SPECIMEN 884 COMPANION

    The Last War

    Earth is dying. Escape cannot carry everyone.

    Colonel Naomi Sato survives nuclear war underground with her daughter, Hana. As Hana fights to keep patients alive, Naomi helps scattered survivors assemble humanity’s last chance to leave Earth.

    But the shelters are failing. The fleet has no destination. And some of the people building it will never have a seat.

    A companion to SPECIMEN 884, The Last War follows a mother and daughter through the collapse of their world—and the impossible choices that stand between those who escape and those left behind.

    A VISUAL COMPANION · GRAPHIC NOVEL

    Vaelen-Khor

    A civilization carved into stone. A friendship caught in its fractures.

    For Ashel and Doruun, Vaelen-Khor is home: familiar passages, ordinary work, and departures that promise a return.

    Then something in its rhythm goes wrong.

    Ashel struggles to keep others alive while the path to his friend falls silent. Years pass. New generations inherit an old wound. And the hands that maintain a civilization must find another way to hold what it has lost.

    A visual companion to SPECIMEN 884, Vaelen-Khor explores friendship, endurance, and the need to leave a trace when the universe offers no answer.
